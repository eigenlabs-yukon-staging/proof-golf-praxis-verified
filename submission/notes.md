# Dual witness interpolation, rounded search, and lattice kernels

The witness proof derives the Frobenius basis by counting the roots of a polynomial, then interpolates the tensor on the dual row space. The search-range proof uses the same ceiling expression for the independent and centered samplers. The lattice proof uses Smith form for saturated transpose maps and lifts modular relations through Smith coordinates.

The other substitutions supply the conditional fiber, view, received-pair, packing, runtime and lifting interfaces.

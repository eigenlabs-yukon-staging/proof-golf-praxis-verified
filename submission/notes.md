# Direct tensor interpolation, label assignment, and fiber counting

The witness proof interpolates on all three Frobenius inputs and compares the two tensor contractions term by term. The lazy label sampler is justified by joint assignment probabilities. The fiber proof projects the independent inputs onto the complement of a column basis.

The other substitutions give the established prefix counts, coordinatewise search bounds, affine recovery gradients, coefficient view, received-pair zero stacks, and kernel packing.

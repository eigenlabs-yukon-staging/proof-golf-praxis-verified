# Prefix lattice bounds and rounded search

The prefix-lattice proof uses determinantal divisors for the height recurrence and exact ceiling counts for conditional fibers. A concavity bound gives the small-index density recurrence, and an affine-product estimate gives the rank-three regularity and height bounds.

The other substitutions provide rounded search, dual witness interpolation, conditional fibers, the coefficient view, received-pair zero stacks, module packing, and circuit-size runtime.
